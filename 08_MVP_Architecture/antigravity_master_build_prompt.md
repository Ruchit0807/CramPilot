\# PROJECT CONTEXT

You are building a production-quality MVP SaaS application called “Exam-Clutch.”

Exam-Clutch is an AI-powered exam survival platform for university students.

The product helps students prepare for exams in 24–48 hours using:

\- AI workflow orchestration

\- professor-aware study strategies

\- syllabus analysis

\- previous year paper analysis

\- prompt generation

\- emergency study roadmaps

\- flashcard workflows

\- revision systems

The platform does NOT replace AI models.

Instead, it intelligently routes students to the BEST AI tool and workflow for each learning task.

Examples:

\- Claude for derivations

\- NotebookLM for flashcards

\- Gemini for large syllabus analysis

\- ChatGPT for viva practice

\- Perplexity for research-heavy subjects

The platform adapts based on:

\- subject type

\- professor behavior

\- time remaining

\- weak topics

\- target marks

\- PYQ patterns
